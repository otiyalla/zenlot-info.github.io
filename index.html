Suggested journal content — write it your way.

In plain language

  • Stop-lossThe price where you accept the trade didn’t work and close it.
  • Take-profitThe price where you take your gain and close the trade.
  • PipThe smallest standard price step of a currency pair.
  • Lot sizeHow much you are trading. Zenlot can recommend one from your risk profile.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Head to support.

Who is Zenlot for?

Traders at every experience level who want to build discipline through rules, trade recording, and reflection. The app focuses on forex.

What can I record?

Trade details such as entry, lot size, stop-loss, and take-profit, together with written notes. Closed trades receive a verdict you can review later.

Does Zenlot place trades with my broker?

No. Zenlot records and tracks trades; it does not submit orders to your broker. A tracked trade can close automatically in the app when its stop-loss or take-profit is reached — that updates your record, it is not a brokerage order.

Is Zenlot free?

Yes. Zenlot is currently free.

Where can I download it?

Use the App Store or Google Play buttons on this page.

Which languages are supported?

English, French and Spanish throughout the app — including dates and number formatting, which follow your device and language settings.

How do I delete my account and data?

Follow the process on our account deletion page.

Is Zenlot financial advice?

No. Zenlot is a tool for applying rules you define yourself and recording your own decisions. It does not provide financial advice, recommendations or trade signals. Trading carries risk, including the loss of your capital.
