The real problem

Most traders don't lose to the market.
They lose to themselves.

You size up after a loss. You move the stop "just this once." You take the profit early because you're nervous, then let the loser run because you're hopeful. None of that is a strategy problem — and none of it shows up in a P&L screenshot.

Sizing drift

Your position size quietly creeps up when you're chasing a loss — and that's the trade that hurts.

Decisions under pressure

Choosing a stop mid-trade means choosing it at the exact moment you're least able to be objective.

Invisible patterns

Without written reasoning, you repeat the same mistake for months without ever recognising it as one mistake.

How it works

Three steps. About a minute to set up.

  1. 01

    Set your risk profile

    Pick Prudent, Balanced or Dynamic — or set every limit by hand. This defines how Zenlot flags a trade as outside your rules.

  2. 02

    Plan before you enter

    Zenlot auto-calculates stop-loss, take-profit and size from your defaults, and tells you immediately when a trade breaks your own limits.

  3. 03

    Journal and review

    Capture why you took it. Then use weekly and monthly pip analysis to see which habits actually pay — and which ones cost you.

Risk profiles

Pick the one that sounds like you.

Your style tells Zenlot how to flag trades, so the journal fits how you actually think. Change it anytime — or go advanced and edit every rule yourself.

Prudent

Smaller positions, steadier swings. Tight limits that keep any single trade from mattering too much.

Balanced

Moderate sizing, mixed risk. A middle ground for traders who want structure without a straitjacket.

Dynamic

Larger positions, bigger swings. Wider limits — still limits, still enforced, still journaled.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Head to support.

Does Zenlot connect to my broker?

No broker connection is required. Zenlot is a planning and journaling layer that sits alongside however you already trade — you keep full control of execution.

Is Zenlot free?

Zenlot is free to download on the App Store and Google Play. Create an account and you can set up your risk profile in about a minute.

Which languages are supported?

English, French and Spanish, throughout the app — including dates, number formatting and decimal separators, which follow your device and language settings.

Can I change my risk rules later?

Anytime. Switch between Prudent, Balanced and Dynamic, or use advanced mode to edit each rule individually starting from your current profile.

How do I delete my account and data?

Follow the process on our account deletion page.

Is Zenlot financial advice?

No. Zenlot is a tool for applying rules you define yourself and recording your own decisions. It does not provide financial advice, recommendations or trade signals. Trading carries risk, including the loss of your capital.
